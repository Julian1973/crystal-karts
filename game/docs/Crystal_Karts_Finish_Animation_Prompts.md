# Crystal Karts — five-second finish animation templates

These are proposed animation templates, not approved render-ready prompts. Attach the exact approved references and a measured five-second audio bed before rendering. No renders have been requested.

Use 16:9 landscape, five seconds, one continuous shot. Select duration and aspect ratio in your provider interface. Export a broadly compatible MP4 for the game. Keep faces and action in the central 70% so the clip can fit phones and tablets.

For reusable victory clips, approve a silent five-second @Audio1 bed and keep mouths non-speaking; the game can play the winner’s existing voice separately. For lip sync, supply the exact character voice clip, fit and approve its five-second timing, then revise the action timing around that waveform. Do not squeeze a longer victory line into five seconds.

Shared podium proposal: a low, rounded three-place podium in Crystal Cove, centre platform highest, pale warm stone with inset crystal-colour trim, soft woodland background and a glimpse of sea. No baked-in winner names, rank labels, logos or text; the game adds those. Approve a single scene plate first and reuse it for all nine characters. Preserve approved character scale; frame the smaller bees more closely rather than enlarging their bodies.
